### 3. Data Quality Risk
- Historical data may contain errors or gaps

### 4. Execution Risk
- Signals are theoretical; actual execution differs

### 5. Technology Risk
- Software bugs may cause incorrect signals
- System outages can miss trading opportunities
- Data feed interruptions affect decisions
- Model drift may degrade performance over time

### 6. Market Risk
- Systematic market crashes affect all stocks
- Black swan events cannot be predicted
- Regulatory changes can impact strategies
- Geopolitical events create volatility
- Economic cycles affect market behavior

## Limitations of Machine Learning Models

### LSTM/GRU Models
- Require large amounts of training data
- Can overfit to historical patterns
- Computationally expensive
- May not generalize to new market conditions
- No theoretical guarantee of accuracy

### Technical Indicators
- Lagging indicators based on past prices
- Generate false signals in ranging markets
- No predictive power guarantee
- Subject to interpretation bias
